Nurul Quran Pipeline

Nurul Quran Pipeline is a personal tool that uploads and manages videos on the Nurul Quran YouTube channel. It is used only by the owner of that channel and is not offered to, or usable by, anyone else.

The tool uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos and to update the channel's own video metadata, thumbnails and playlists.
